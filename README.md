## The scene

> Dad burned the toast again, so Mum called it smoked and served it on the good plates. Grandma, on video call from the village, held her phone upside down and clapped anyway.

A small domestic moment: burnt toast rescued as "smoked", served on the good plates, while Grandma cheers from an upside-down video call. Warm, light, and specific — the kind of line a family would actually laugh at.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the scene to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), with no trailing newline. The entry is one line of 173 characters, inside the 1–280 character gate.
